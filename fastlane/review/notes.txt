Biscuit is a shared care log for a pet: meals, walks, potty breaks, litter and medications, each saved with the person and time. There is no account of any kind, so no demo account is needed.

FRESH INSTALL
1. Enter your name and a pet name, pick the animal, tap Continue.
2. The Household screen offers the subscription with a 7-day free trial. Tap Get Started below the button to continue free.
3. Now shows four large log buttons. One tap logs the entry with your name and the time. Undo, corrections and deletions add a new entry that names the old one, so history is never rewritten.
4. History lists every entry and correction. Care holds user-entered medications and recurring items with local reminders. Settings has export (CSV) and the household options.

FREE AND PAID
Free: one pet, solo logging, full history, reminders and export. Household subscription (monthly or yearly, 7-day free trial on both): sharing with other iPhones through iCloud, extra pets, and a one-page sitter sheet PDF. The owner subscribes; people who join the household pay nothing and never see a purchase screen. History and export stay available after a subscription ends.

SHARING
Settings > Household > Invite (after subscribing) prepares an iCloud share and shows a link and QR code. The second iPhone joins from the link, or from Join a household on the first screen. Testing a join needs a second iPhone signed in to a different iCloud account.

MEDICATIONS
Medications and doses are entered by the user. The app gives no dosing, diagnosis or treatment advice and states that the veterinarian directs care. A medication reminder opens Now, which shows the last recorded dose, who gave it and when the log last synced.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Apple iCloud (CloudKit) stores and shares the household log. StoreKit handles the subscriptions, and RevenueCat checks subscription status. There is no developer server, account system, advertising or AI.

REGIONS
The app works the same in every region.
