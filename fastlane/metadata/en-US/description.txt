Everyone caring for your pet sees what happened and what is next.

Log potty breaks, meals, walks and medications with the person and time attached. Check Now for the last of each, the last successful sync time and the recurring items you entered.

For a new puppy or an older dog, keep the daily record together. Cats, rabbits, small pets, birds, fish and reptiles each get their own buttons, like litter, cleaning or water.

FREE
One pet, solo logging, full history, entered reminders and CSV export. Corrections stay in the record. Your history and export remain available when a subscription ends.

HOUSEHOLD
Share a log by iCloud invitation link or QR code, add extra pets and create a one-page sitter sheet PDF. The owner subscribes, and everyone joining is covered. Monthly and yearly plans each offer a 7-day free trial to eligible subscribers. Plans renew automatically unless canceled through App Store Settings.

Live household sharing requires iPhones and iCloud. Helpers on Android can receive a sitter sheet PDF snapshot. A disconnected phone displays stale status. Medication reminders belong to one chosen iPhone and open Now so you can check the last dose and sync time.

Biscuit is a record keeper. Your veterinarian directs your pet's care.
