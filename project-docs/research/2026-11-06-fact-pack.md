## Hard constraints
- App Review 1.4.1: no diagnosis, treatment or dosing advice; keep a disclaimer. House rule: must not look vibe coded, no taglines or helper text, simplest possible UX.
- Solo owner with an AI fleet: build cost is low but each app adds maintenance. Scope discipline is a requirement.
- CloudKit sharing already exists in the Vehicle Log; a Supabase backend does not carry over.
- No spending without asking.

## Draft plan to attack (Claude's first pass; not a conclusion)
1. **Pitch:** shared pet care for households: "who gave the pill, what is due next." Not a records vault. Reliable sync and no account are the promise, the top complaints of paid competitors.
2. **Use case:** two or more people caring for one or more pets, with a chronic-condition or senior pet as the most motivated buyer. The recurring job is a daily or monthly med/preventive that must not be doubled or missed. Records and emergency info come along as the second layer, not the front door.
3. **Scope v1:** pets, due list, meds with who-gave-it, entries with photo, weight, emergency card, PDF, share by link, reminders, widget. Cut OCR, cost insights, CSV prominence, breed catalogs, onboarding beyond adding a pet.
4. **ASO:** title and subtitle around pet tracker, pet log, dog log, pet health, pet diary, dog medication tracker, cat tracker. Avoid every floor term in visible fields. Name unresolved.
5. **Paid:** free: unlimited logging and sharing for 1 pet... or 2 pets as in the Vehicle Log. Pro: extra pets, PDF, widget, etc. Prices near $2.99/mo with trial, $19.99/yr with trial, $39.99 lifetime. Open question: gate on pets, on household sharing, or on PDF.
6. **TikTok:** a topic page first (vet and pet-owner realities, senior dog routines, what a boarding kennel asks for, pill humor), app mentioned later. Experiment across 12 formats at 3 posts/day on a new account. Possibly paid vet-tech creator test only with Jack's approval.
7. **Go/no-go:** build v1 at low cost, then read: install rate on target terms, activation, second-person invites, trial starts. Kill or fold if not met.
