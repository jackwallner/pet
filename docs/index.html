Biscuit: Critter Log

A daily record for everyone caring for your pet.

Log potty breaks, meals, walks and medications, with the person and time attached. Live sharing uses iPhone and iCloud. Dogs, cats, rabbits, small pets, birds, fish and reptiles are supported.

Free includes one pet, solo logging, history, reminders and export. Household adds sharing, extra pets and a one-page sitter sheet PDF.
