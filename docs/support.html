Sharing

Both people need an iPhone signed in to iCloud. The household owner sends an invitation from Settings, Household, Invite. The other person opens the link, scans its QR code or pastes the link into Join household.

Stale records

Now shows the last successful sync time. Check the connection and iCloud availability, then open the app on both phones and allow time for import. Confirm recent medication records with the person caring for the pet.

Medication reminders

Each medication has one reminder phone. Open Care, Medications, Edit medication to select this iPhone. Notification permission is controlled in iPhone Settings. Tapping a medication reminder opens Now.

Export and access

Settings, Export log creates a CSV with the full correction record. History and export remain available after subscription expiry. Household owners can restore purchases from Settings, Household.
