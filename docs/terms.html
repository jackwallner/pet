The Apple standard end user license agreement governs the iPhone app.

Care records

Biscuit is a record keeper. Your veterinarian directs your pet's care. Enter schedules and instructions supplied for your pet. Check with the household before acting on a medication record. CloudKit delivery can be delayed, and disconnected phones show stale status. A PDF represents the moment it was created.

Subscriptions

The free tier includes one pet, solo logging, history, reminders and export. Household covers live iCloud sharing, extra pets and sitter sheet PDFs. The owner purchases access for everyone joining. Prices, trial eligibility and renewal terms appear in the app and Apple's purchase confirmation. Manage or cancel subscriptions through App Store Settings.

On expiry, all records remain viewable and exportable. The owner retains solo logging on the first pet. Participants may write for 14 days after household expiry and then have read-only access. Renewal or restoration resumes access.

Sharing

Anyone with an open invitation link can join. Share links with people you trust. The owner can manage or revoke sharing through the app's sharing controls.
