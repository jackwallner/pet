Privacy

Updated November 6, 2026

Your records

Pet profiles, medication instructions, recurring items and attributed log entries are stored on your iPhone. With iCloud available, Apple stores and syncs the log using CloudKit. A household invitation shares the household's records with the people who join. Anyone holding an open invitation link can join and add entries.

Corrections, undo and deletions create new history entries. The original record stays in the log's correction history and exports. Stopping a share removes access for its participants. You can export your records before leaving a household or removing the app.

Purchases and measurement

Apple processes subscriptions. RevenueCat manages purchase status and receives a pseudonymous customer identifier, purchase receipts, subscription information, app and device information, and our usage attributes. These attributes contain counts, dates, product identifiers and fixed screen names: app opens, paywall views, conversion information, first log date, logging on day seven, invitation taps, sent invitations and share joins. Pet names, medication names, instructions and other text you enter stay outside those measurement attributes.

Apple and RevenueCat may process information on servers outside your country. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders and exports

Reminders are local notifications on your iPhone. A medication's selected reminder phone is part of its household settings. CSV exports contain the full correction record. Sitter sheet PDFs are snapshots and may contain pet and medication information. You choose where to send exported files.

Your choices

iCloud sharing and notifications are optional. Manage iCloud data through Apple Settings. Deleting the app removes its local files; iCloud data and previously shared exports may remain with Apple or their recipients. Contact support for help with your data or RevenueCat customer information.
